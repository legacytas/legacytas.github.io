About us

A booking desk built in Port Moresby, for PNG travellers

Legacy Travel and Services is an online travel service based in Port Moresby, Papua New Guinea, serving clients in all 22 provinces. We book domestic and international flights, and handle the paperwork that goes with travel — tourist and business visa lodgement, biometrics appointments, and passport applications.

We're a small local team — we know the routes people actually fly, the documents each visa office actually wants, and the questions first-time applicants ask, whether you're booking from Port Moresby or a provincial centre. That's what we've built the service around.
